PRESERVED, WITH CURIOSITY

A student project,
a new life in the browser.

Originally created in 2019 by Weihang Ren and Zhongyu Zhang for IEORE4501 Tools for Analytics, this project explored the 2018 Central Park Squirrel Census. This exhibit preserves those observations as a read-only archive.

Read the observations with care.

A sighting is an observation, not an individually tracked animal. Fur colors and behaviors are reported by observers. Missing information stays “Unknown”; overlapping activities can total more than 100%.

The original CSV is included unchanged. The map plots its longitude and latitude over the current NYC Parks property boundary, retrieved in September 2026. This boundary is context, not a reconstruction of the 2018 survey area; all observations are retained, including those just outside it. This archive does not collect new sightings.
